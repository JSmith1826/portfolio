Background

A technical mindset shaped by operations.

I know what happens when the record is incomplete, the process lives in one person’s head, or the tool doesn’t fit the work.

My career began in hospitality, including seven years in banquet and restaurant operations at Michigan State University’s Kellogg Hotel & Conference Center. I later worked across restaurants, specialty food distribution, outdoor recreation, and other small businesses. Along the way I took on purchasing, inventory, customer service, staff training, documentation, and systems work.

MSU’s Data Analytics Boot Camp gave me more formal practice with Python, SQL, JavaScript, and visualization. I use those tools alongside Excel and Access when they suit the problem. I am especially interested in reporting, records and data quality, operations analysis, implementation support, and practical automation.
