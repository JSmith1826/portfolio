Short exploratory project

A historical data question, explored in an afternoon.

I combined line-of-duty death records with law-enforcement staffing data to test ways of comparing incidents across states and causes.

The work

The notebook gathers historical records from the Officer Down Memorial Page, cleans incident fields, and exports tables for visual analysis. A second notebook prepares state-level comparison data. The chart shown below covers 1992–2018.

It was a quick visual study, not a maintained public dataset. I include it to show how I approach an unfamiliar source and turn it into a question worth investigating further.

How to read this chart

The original graphic uses the phrase “deadliest states.” Its rates depend on historical staffing figures and choices made while aligning sources. They should be treated as exploratory estimates, not a current or definitive ranking of risk for officers in any state.

The underlying records, time coverage, and denominator deserve a more complete methods review before drawing stronger conclusions.
